About ShadowWatch AI

Intelligent defense for a hostile digital world

Project Description

ShadowWatch AI is a next-generation security operations platform that fuses machine learning, global threat feeds and behavioral analytics to detect, prioritize and respond to cyber attacks in real time — across endpoints, networks and cloud workloads.


Our Mission

To make enterprise-grade threat intelligence accessible, actionable and instant, so every organization can see danger before it strikes.

Key Benefits

  • Detect threats in milliseconds with AI models
  • Reduce false positives by up to 90%
  • Unified visibility across all assets
  • Automated risk scoring and response
  • Compliance-ready audit reporting
  • Scales from startups to enterprises
